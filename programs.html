Our Programs

Tuition Programs for CBSE, ICSE, SSC & IB Students

Expert Maths & Science coaching for Std 3rd to 9th, aligned to your child's exact board syllabus — CBSE, ICSE, SSC (Maharashtra State Board) and IB/IGCSE — in Kalwa, Kharegaon, Parsik Nagar and nearby Thane areas.

Every board teaches Maths and Science a little differently — the syllabus depth, exam pattern and pace all vary.
At Usha Tutorials, we don't use one generic teaching style for every student. Each program below is built around what that specific board actually expects, while keeping our core approach the same across all of them: small batches, concept-first teaching, regular doubt-solving and consistent progress tracking.

All programs are available for Std 3rd to 9th and focus on Mathematics and Science. Jump to the board that applies to your child, or read through all four to see which teaching style might be the best fit.

Program

CBSE Tuition Classes (Std 3rd-9th)

What's Covered

Our CBSE program follows the NCERT-based curriculum for Mathematics and Science, chapter by chapter, in step with what your child is currently studying at school. We cover concept explanation, in-class problem solving, chapter-end practice and periodic revision — building toward both unit tests and final exams.

Our Teaching Approach

CBSE increasingly tests application and understanding rather than memorisation, through case-study and assertion-reason style questions. So instead of just working through textbook exercises, we focus on why a concept works, not just how to apply it — using real-world examples, model-making and guided problem-solving so students can handle unfamiliar question formats confidently, not just repeat what they've memorised.

Who It's For

Students who grasp concepts quickly and enjoy problem-solving tend to do especially well with CBSE's application-first style. It's also a strong fit for families who may relocate cities in the coming years, since CBSE's curriculum is consistent across India.

Program

ICSE Tuition Classes (Std 3rd-9th)

What's Covered

ICSE's Maths and Science syllabus is detailed and thorough, often covering topics in greater depth than other boards at the same standard. Our ICSE program works through the full breadth of the syllabus with structured notes, worked examples and regular practice tests, so nothing feels rushed or skipped over.

Our Teaching Approach

Because ICSE rewards precision and complete, well-structured answers, we place extra emphasis on answer-writing practice alongside concept teaching — how to present a derivation, label a diagram correctly, or structure a full-mark science answer. Combined with periodic tests, this builds the discipline ICSE exams specifically expect.

Who It's For

A great fit for students who benefit from thorough, step-by-step explanations and don't mind a slightly heavier workload in exchange for a very strong academic foundation in Maths and Science fundamentals.

Program

SSC (Maharashtra State Board) Tuition Classes (Std 3rd-9th)

What's Covered

Our SSC program follows the Maharashtra State Board syllabus for Mathematics and Science, with direct, syllabus-aligned coverage of every chapter. We match our pacing to the school's own timeline, so students stay ahead of class tests instead of catching up after the fact.

Our Teaching Approach

SSC exams tend to follow a more predictable, direct question pattern with clear weightage per topic. We take advantage of that structure — focused revision around high-weightage topics, regular unit tests that mirror the real exam pattern, and consistent doubt-solving so small gaps don't compound into bigger ones closer to exams.

Who It's For

Students who benefit from a clear, predictable exam structure, and families who value strong local relevance — SSC remains closely tied to opportunities within Maharashtra. We also support students switching into or out of SSC from another board, with a short bridge plan to close any syllabus gaps.

Program

IB / IGCSE Tuition Classes (Std 3rd-9th)

What's Covered

Our IB/IGCSE program supports the inquiry-based Maths and Science curriculum these boards follow, with coverage built around your child's specific school syllabus rather than a single fixed textbook — since IB and IGCSE schools often vary more in their exact materials than CBSE or SSC schools do.

Our Teaching Approach

IB and IGCSE reward inquiry, investigation and applying concepts to open-ended problems rather than repeating fixed procedures. We lean into that with guided investigations, real-world application questions, and discussion-based sessions that build the kind of independent thinking these boards specifically assess.

Who It's For

Best suited to students who enjoy open-ended problem-solving and explaining their reasoning, rather than following a single fixed method — and for families who want a globally consistent curriculum, especially if international relocation is a possibility.
